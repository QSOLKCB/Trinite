# Curated public-domain source candidates

Requested by Trent during training development. Status: **source shortlist,
not an admitted text corpus**. The listed Project Gutenberg catalogue records
were reviewed on 2026-10-09 and mark these editions public domain in the USA.
That status is not an Australian or worldwide rights determination. Pin actual
file bytes, source edition, author/translator/editor/illustrator details,
acquisition terms and jurisdiction evidence before training or distribution.
Follow [DATA.md](DATA.md) and [ethics intake](ETHICS.md).

| Work / exact catalogue record | Proposed curriculum use | Preparation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| John Stuart Mill, [Utilitarianism, #11224](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11224) | Consequences, competing interests and comparison of ethical arguments | Select short source-bound passages; preserve perspective and disagreements; create fresh scenario evaluation |
| Immanuel Kant, [Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals, #5682](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682), Thomas Kingsmill Abbott translation | Duties, principles, human dignity and contrasts with consequentialist arguments | Pin this translation separately; do not substitute a modern translation or commentary; annotate source/argument rather than moral gold labels |
| George Boole, [An Investigation of the Laws of Thought, #15114](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/15114) | Topic planning for exact logic/probability exercises | Inspect the TeX/PDF mathematical transcription; generate/check new exact exercises independently; historical notation is not an executable oracle |
| Mary Shelley, [Frankenstein, #84](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/84) | Responsibility, consequences, perspectives and fiction-versus-reality classification | Explicit fiction tags; select a specific 1818/1831 edition before admission; the catalogue points to improved edition records rather than guaranteeing one interchangeable text |

Start with a small, balanced set rather than whole-book memorisation. A proposed
sequence is newly generated exact logic first, then a limited Mill/Kant contrast,
then narrative tasks with fiction labels. These are curriculum judgments, not
measured model benefits. Public-domain status says nothing about truth,
scientific currency, bias, safety or evaluation freshness. Neither game-theory
expertise nor realistic ethical judgment follows from reading these works.

[Project Gutenberg's reuse guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html)
explains its US rights scope and separate terms/trademark requirements. Retain
source attribution and rights evidence, review every actual ebook/header, and
record any normalization or removal of publisher/web boilerplate explicitly.
Do not ingest automatically generated catalogue summaries, modern introductions,
new translations or unrelated web content as though they were the historical
public-domain work. No books are downloaded or admitted by this shortlist.

Harvard's collection remains a separate requested-access source with its own
agreement and scope, recorded in [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md#harvard-public-domain-intake).
Do not use the catalogue or this shortlist as a substitute for per-source
admission and exposure receipts. Keep fresh assessment scenarios and answer keys
out of training, including all paraphrases and related narrative/argument families.
