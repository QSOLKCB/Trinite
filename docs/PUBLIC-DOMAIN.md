## Curriculum and acquisition boundaries

Start with a small, balanced set rather than whole-book memorisation. A proposed
sequence is newly generated exact logic first, then a limited Mill/Kant contrast,
then narrative tasks with fiction labels. These are curriculum judgments, not
measured model benefits. Public-domain status says nothing about truth,
scientific currency, bias, safety or evaluation freshness. Neither game-theory
expertise nor realistic ethical judgment follows from reading these works.

[Project Gutenberg's reuse guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html)
explains its US rights scope and separate terms/trademark requirements. Retain
source attribution and rights evidence, review every actual ebook/header, and
record any normalization or removal of publisher/web boilerplate explicitly.
Do not ingest automatically generated catalogue summaries, modern introductions,
new translations or unrelated web content as though they were the historical
public-domain work. No books are downloaded or admitted by this shortlist.

Harvard's collection remains a separate requested-access source with its own
agreement and scope, recorded in [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md#harvard-public-domain-intake).
Do not use the catalogue or this shortlist as a substitute for per-source
admission and exposure receipts. Keep fresh assessment scenarios and answer keys
out of training, including all paraphrases and related narrative/argument families.
