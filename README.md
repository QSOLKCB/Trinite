An inspectable, ternary-weight edge AI research project: small models, curated data, geometric reasoning experiments, and PROVENANCE observation.

**Status: Phase 4 geometry measurement implemented.** Matched dense and ternary CPU decoders support native training, safe replay, detached prompt captures, pinned QSOL-GEO-REASON analysis and PROVENANCE observation. The [research roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) retains the complete comparison and learning experiments; original fold/composed-output learning adequacy, format selection and Phase 5 remain blocked.

The [finite maths curriculum](docs/MATHS.md) adds independently checked finite maths and exact linear algebra. Its two corrected dense pilot seeds improve maths counts but regress arithmetic training retention. The next [balanced rehearsal experiment](docs/MATHS-RETENTION.md) completed four verified runs: arithmetic training accuracy rises to 97.0% and 99.6%, with a maths accuracy tradeoff. Reused test results remain exploratory and drift prevention is unresolved.

The separate [external reference geometry comparison](docs/REFERENCE-GEOMETRY.md) retains pinned Qwen stock/profile activation captures and independent arithmetic checks. Geometry observations are descriptive; intervention, fresh capability evidence and edge benchmarks remain pending. See [Phase 4 evidence and limits](docs/PHASE-4.md).
