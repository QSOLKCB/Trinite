An inspectable, ternary-weight edge AI research project: small models, curated data, geometric reasoning experiments, and PROVENANCE observation.

**Status: Phase 4 geometry measurement implemented.** Matched dense and ternary CPU decoders support native training, safe replay, detached prompt captures, pinned QSOL-GEO-REASON analysis and PROVENANCE observation. The [research roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) retains the complete comparison and learning experiments; original fold/composed-output learning adequacy, format selection and Phase 5 remain blocked.

The [finite maths curriculum](docs/MATHS.md) adds independently checked finite maths and exact linear algebra. Its two corrected dense pilot seeds improve maths counts but regress arithmetic training retention. The next [balanced rehearsal experiment](docs/MATHS-RETENTION.md) completed four verified runs: arithmetic training accuracy rises to 97.0% and 99.6%, with a maths accuracy tradeoff. Reused test results remain exploratory and drift prevention is unresolved. The [v2 assurance increment](docs/MATHS-RETENTION-V2.md) separates final inference from complete optimizer replay and strengthens evidence/worker integration coverage. The [training-only budget comparison](docs/MATHS-RETENTION-BUDGET.md) completed four matched 4,096/8,192-update runs with complete optimizer replay. Maths improves in both seeds and arithmetic passes, but every maths task remains below its 90% gate. The next [task-balanced scheduling increment](docs/MATHS-TASK-BALANCE.md) tests equal maths-task exposure at the same 8,192-update budget, preserving the prior producer and results.

The independent [recursive 3×3×3 lattice scaffold](docs/LATTICE-SCAFFOLD.md) fixes a bounded integer Menger-style address/adjacency reference and replayable source-bound artifacts. A separate [native activation-to-lattice observation](docs/ACTIVATION-LATTICE.md) compares previously retained trained activation captures with four matched controls; this is retrospective and does not fit a neural model, prove useful learned geometry or enable geometry loss. The separate [external reference geometry comparison](docs/REFERENCE-GEOMETRY.md) retains pinned Qwen stock/profile activation captures and independent arithmetic checks. Geometry observations are descriptive; intervention, fresh capability evidence and edge benchmarks remain pending. See [Phase 4 evidence and limits](docs/PHASE-4.md).
